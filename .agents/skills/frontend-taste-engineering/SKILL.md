# Frontend Taste Engineering (Anti-Slop Framework)

Deliver distinctive landing pages, portfolios, web apps, and redesigns free of generic AI templates. Always perform a **Design Read** of the brief first, calibrate the three dials, and lock semantic tokens before writing UI code.

## 1. The 3 Calibration Dials
- **`DESIGN_VARIANCE` (1-10):** Layout asymmetry (`7-9` expressive/editorial, `4-6` structured SaaS, `1-3` strict utility).
- **`MOTION_INTENSITY` (1-10):** Spring choreography (`6-8` narrative marketing, `3-4` workspace tools, `1` reduced-motion).
- **`VISUAL_DENSITY` (1-10):** Information density (`3-4` generous whitespace, `7-8` telemetry/cockpit dashboards).

## 2. Core Anti-Slop Rules
- **Token & Color Lock:** Anchor to project tokens first; lock 1 primary accent (`<80%` saturation) across the entire page; ban purple/indigo AI glow gradients and `#000000` pure black.
- **Typography & Punctuation:** Prefer distinctive fonts (`Geist`, `Satoshi`, `Cabinet Grotesk`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`); enforce `tabular-nums` on metrics; strictly ban em-dashes (`—`) and en-dashes (`–`) in UI copy.
- **Viewport & Ergonomics:** Use `min-h-[100dvh]` (never `100vh`), `48x48px` mobile touch targets, GPU-safe animations (`transform`/`opacity` only), and all 6 interactive component states.
