# Marketing Psychology: Behavioral Economics for UI

Apply proven behavioral science principles to reduce decision paralysis and increase user engagement ethically.

## 1. Core Decision Principles
- **1.A Loss Aversion:** Users feel losses 2x more intensely than equivalent gains. Frame copy around what the user stops losing (*"Stop losing leads to slow support"* converts better than *"Improve your support"*).
- **1.B Price Anchoring:** The first number seen sets the mental anchor. Present the Enterprise/high tier or original strikethrough price first so the recommended Pro tier reads as high value.
- **1.C Hick's Law & Choice Paradox:** More choices increase cognitive load and drop conversion. Cap pricing tiers at **3 options** (*Starter, Pro, Enterprise*), badge one as **"Most Popular"**, and avoid competing primary CTAs in the same viewport.

## 2. Ethical Persuasion Principles (Cialdini)
- **2.A Reciprocity:** Deliver upfront value (interactive calculators, templates, no-card sandbox) before asking for signup or payment.
- **2.B Social Proof:** Place active user counts (`15,000+ teams`), review ratings (`4.9/5 on G2`), and role-specific testimonials directly beside conversion points.
- **2.C Authority:** Display security/compliance badges (`SOC2`, `ISO 27001`, `GDPR/LGPD`), press logos, and domain expert endorsements.

## 3. Onboarding & Engagement Psychology
- **3.A IKEA / Endowment Effect:** Let users customize one meaningful element during onboarding (workspace theme, logo, first template) to build ownership.
- **3.B Goal-Gradient Effect:** Start multi-step onboarding progress bars at `25%` complete (*"Step 1 of 3 complete"*).
- **3.C Zeigarnik Effect:** Use concise setup checklists (*"Workspace 80% complete — connect your repository to unlock CI alerts"*).
