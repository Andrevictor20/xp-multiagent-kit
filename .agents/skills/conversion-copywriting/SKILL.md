# Conversion Copywriting

Write clear, persuasive, action-oriented interface and landing page copy where every word earns its place.

## 1. Core Copywriting Principles
1. **Clarity Over Cleverness:** Visitors must grasp the offer in `< 3s`. Clear copy outperforms clever puns by up to 81%.
2. **Benefits > Features (The "So What?" Bridge):**
   - *Feature:* "AES-256 encryption with 50ms sync."
   - *Benefit:* "Keep confidential files 100% leak-proof and synced instantly across every device."
3. **Specificity > Vagueness:**
   - *Vague:* "Save time on your workflow."
   - *Specific:* "Cut weekly financial reporting from 4 hours to 15 minutes."
4. **Customer Voice > Corporate Jargon:** Ban "synergy", "cutting-edge", "innovative platform". Use the exact words customers use in support tickets and reviews.
5. **One Idea Per Section:** Each section defends and proves a single core argument.

## 2. High-Converting Hero Headline Formulas (H1)
1. **[Desired Outcome] Without [Primary Pain]:** *"Launch high-converting pages without touching a line of code."*
2. **The [Category] for [Target Audience]:** *"The agile CRM engineered specifically for product teams."*
3. **Permanent Frustration Elimination:** *"Never lose a qualified lead to slow response times again."*
4. **Diagnostic Question:** *"Tired of spending Friday afternoons formatting financial spreadsheets?"*
- **Supporting Subheadline:** Expand the *how* in **<= 20 words** (2-3 short lines).

## 3. High-Response CTA Formula
`[Action Verb] + [What the User Gets] + [Risk Reversal]`

| Weak (Ban) | High-Conversion (Use) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Submit / Register | **Create My Free Account** (No credit card required) |
| Download | **Download the Complete PDF Guide** |
| Learn More | **Watch the 2-Minute Interactive Demo** |
| Buy Now | **Claim Lifetime Access with 30% Off** |
| Free Trial | **Start My 14-Day Free Trial** |

## 4. 7-Section Page Narrative Arc
1. **Hero (Above the Fold):** Impact H1 + <=20-word subheadline + primary value CTA + initial trust logos.
2. **Problem Agitation:** Articulate the user's current workflow pain.
3. **Solution & Benefits:** 3-4 benefit pillars using the "So What?" bridge.
4. **How It Works:** 3 simple steps (`Step 1 -> Step 2 -> Step 3`).
5. **Deep Social Proof:** Named testimonials with real photos, roles, and quantified outcomes.
6. **Objection-Busting FAQ:** Direct answers on pricing, security, migration, and support.
7. **Final CTA & Risk Reversal:** Repeat primary CTA paired with money-back guarantee or cancel-anytime assurance.

## 5. UX Microcopy
- **Input Placeholders:** Concrete examples (`alex@company.com`), never generic labels.
- **Trust Microcopy Near CTA:** *"Takes under 60 seconds • Cancel anytime • No credit card required"*.
- **Error Messages:** Calmly state what went wrong and the exact action to fix it (no raw error codes).
